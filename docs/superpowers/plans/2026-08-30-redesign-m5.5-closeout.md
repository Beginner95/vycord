## 4. The whole-branch review, and the seam it found

Four milestones running, this seat has found defects no per-task reviewer could see. It did so again.

**T4's mousedown opt-out silently removed cross-picker dismissal.** T4 fixed the one-way toggle by adding
`onMouseDown={(e) => e.stopPropagation()}` to the composer's sticker and emoji buttons — correct for its own
purpose. But React's `SyntheticEvent.stopPropagation()` calls `nativeEvent.stopPropagation()` at the root
container, so the event never reaches the **document** `mousedown` listener — which is also what dismisses
*the other* picker.

Both pickers are `position:absolute; right:12px; bottom:calc(100% + 8px); z-index:30`; emoji is 264×240 and
sticker 324×320, so **emoji sits entirely inside sticker's extent**, and the sticker paints on top regardless
of open order. Emoji→sticker left a ghost panel revealed when the sticker closed; **sticker→emoji produced no
visible change at all — a silently dead button.** `Composer.tsx` had **0** `onMouseDown` at T3 and 2 after T4.

Fixed in `663ac4d` by a single `togglePicker('emoji'|'sticker'|'attach')` routed through all four toggles,
**keeping** the `stopPropagation` opt-outs, which are what fix the one-way toggle. `fmtOpen`/`linkOpen` were
deliberately excluded because the formatting toolbar renders the second emoji toggle.

The fix wave also corrected ten record defects, including two stale line citations in committed comments
that `b251fb8` had invalidated (one of them **already wrong in the commit that wrote it**), a false claim
that `ContextMenu` consumes `useDismissOnOutside`, and a backlog entry saying "no `setTimeout`" when `api.ts`
has it at `:59` and `:141` — ironic in an entry whose whole point is that a literally-true-but-misleading
statement must be reworded.

**The re-review executed rather than reasoned.** It ran the exclusion probe at HEAD, then reverted only the
four toggle handlers and re-ran, reproducing `PROBE ERROR: SEAM DEFECT: composer pickers are not mutually
exclusive — 10/10 pairs leave the first picker open` **byte-identically to the fix wave's own fail-first
capture** — independently confirming that capture was genuine rather than reconstructed. Restored, diff
empty, probe green again. All `probe-overlay-contract` companions re-run green, `fmt-emoji: ok` validating
the `fmtOpen` exclusion decision. **Zero residue.**

### The counting method's limits — all four matter for M6

1. **`getMessages` takes `limit`/`offset` only; default `limit` is 50.** An unpaginated read of `#general`

2. **There is no `before` cursor.** A `before=` parameter is **silently ignored** and the server re-reports
   page 1. An early counter used one and reported `#general` as 100 — 50 counted twice.
3. **The search `.total` cross-check is per-channel and substring-based.** Good for the noise ratio, bad for
   the count.
4. **None of these sees attachments.** They must be read separately.

## 9. New debt

**The harness is gitignored *and* ungated, and renames silently disarm it.** T5's `.lightbox` rename had to
re-point **17 selector sites across 4 probe files**, and **two were *negative* preconditions that would have
gone permanently true** — no error, no failing test; the probe simply stops being able to fail. Three other
sites used `?? fail(…)` and *would* have failed loudly. **That asymmetry is the whole lesson.**

**`probe-composer.js:193` is a silently dead gate** — a raw `querySelector('.link-dialog-submit')` that
throws on null, so **48 of its 88 `check()` sites have never executed** since M4 renamed that class in
`ea7b52c`. A 508-line standing gate cited as cross-surface regression coverage, providing false assurance for
two milestones. **The highest-value harness repair on the branch.**

**A diagnostic was nearly certified as a gate.** `probe-lightbox-escape.js` has 0 `fail()` and 0 `throw`; its
own line 1 calls it a diagnostic. A T7 draft grouped it with the real gates in the one table whose entire
function is separating the two — two rows below the probes already listed for exactly that. Caught before
commit. **A diagnostic written mid-task to find a bug reads, three days later, exactly like a gate written to
prevent it**: same directory, same naming, same invocation, and it "passes". *Suggested convention for M6:
name diagnostics `diag-*`.*

**A trunk defect the redesign found and fixed.** develop ships an app-wide Escape swallower —
`useDismissOnOutside` (VYC-81) registers a capture-phase document `keydown` that stops Escape's propagation,
and VYC-82's `AttachmentButton` calls it from the **always-mounted component body**. On develop, with any
channel open, Escape never reaches document-bubble: `MediaLightbox`, the whole `useModalFocus` stack,
`MessageSearch`, and React's delegated `onKeyDown` at `#root` all lose the key. **It produced no console
error, no failing test and no visual difference**, and took three rounds of diagnosis. It was caught only
because a probe asserted the Escape path through a **throwing** `fail()`. *Worth remembering the next time a
step is tempted to record rather than assert.*

Also carried into the RESUME: `onDiscard` orphans blobs but **bounded and self-healing** (a ≤24h deferral,
not a leak — the janitor's orphan branch and `deleteAttachment` share a predicate) · `ChatArea` re-renders on
every upload progress tick · backlog **§1f** (no request timeout; no `signal` on `request()`; the avatar
upload's consequent no-exit trap), which also resolves §6d's cross-reference that previously pointed at
nothing.

## 12. Process notes worth keeping

1. **The instruments were less reliable than the code.** The three most valuable findings were not bugs in
   the feature but failures in the machinery meant to catch bugs — a probe disarmed by a rename (twice), a
   standing gate with 48 unreachable assertions, and a diagnostic nearly certified as a gate. None would ever
   have shown up as a failing test, because their failure mode *is* not failing.
2. **Implementers pushed back on every task, and were right nearly every time** — including on three of my
   own figures and two of my own instructions. The two instructions I got wrong were both caught by the
   person receiving them, not by me.
3. **"Leave it deliberately broken" bought a real signal.** Three rulings kept the attach button unstyled
   specifically so T5 would have something to fail against. T5's fail-first then measured it at the browser's
   default UA style. Had I kept my original ruling, that signal would have been silently absent.
4. **The whole-branch review seat earned its place for the fifth consecutive milestone**, and the defect it
   found was invisible in every single-task diff: a correct fix in one task removing a behaviour that
   depended on the absence of that fix.
5. **A closeout written from a report is a closeout written from a summary.** Given finding 8's pattern,
   every figure in this document was either measured during execution and independently reviewed, or is
   marked as reasoned.
